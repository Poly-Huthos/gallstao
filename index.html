Everything here is observed, not forecast. Temperature, humidity and visibility come from SMHI's weather station at Bromma, about 10 km away, updated every hour. Visibility over 30 km with dry air means clean, transparent air; falling visibility and high humidity mean haze or mist. The verdict uses Meteosat's cloud mask (a new scan every 15 minutes, about 5 km per pixel this far north) at Gällstaö and 8 points around it. Detail images come from Meteosat-12 every 10 minutes. Times are Swedish local time. Satellite data arrives roughly 20–30 minutes after the scan.
Data: EUMETSAT, SMHI.
